# Story Writer: rejection checklist

Answer each question yes or no. Any "yes" rejects the item. Quote the offending line and its scene ID in the verdict. This list can only reject; passing it does not prove quality.

Source labels: `[F#]` refers to a finding in the master library's `research/story-writer.md`; `[proposal]` means no opened source supports it yet.

| # | Reject if yes | Fail example | Pass example | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does a character state their feeling or motive outright? | `I'm angry because you never believed in me.` | `You kept the receipt. Of course you did.` | [F1] |
| 2 | Does the narration or a character state the theme or lesson? | `And so she learned that courage means facing your fears.` | Final image: she leaves the lamp lit and walks out | [F4] |
| 3 | Does the scene start with greetings, arrivals, or setup before the conflict? | `Hi, Tom. — Hi. Coffee? — Sure.` | Scene opens on: `You opened it.` | [F2] |
| 4 | Is the `Turn:` line empty, or the same state as the scene's start? | `Turn: they keep talking about the plan` | `Turn: he now knows she lied about the date` | [proposal] |
| 5 | Can a dialogue line be removed with nothing lost? | `Well, I guess that's that, then.` | Line cut | [F3] |
| 6 | Does a line use the pattern "not X, but Y" or "it wasn't X, it was Y" to sound profound? | `It wasn't a lighthouse. It was a promise.` | `The lamp had not been lit since March.` | [F5] |
| 7 | Are there three or more lists of exactly three in the script (triple adjectives, triple clauses)? | `cold, dark, and silent` / `she ran, she hid, she waited` | Vary: one detail, or two | [F5] |
| 8 | Is an image vague where a specific one was available? | `an old house full of memories` | `a house with a child's heights pencilled on the door frame` | [F6] |
| 9 | Does an action line describe what cannot be seen or heard? | `She remembers her mother's warning.` | `She touches the scar on her wrist.` | [proposal] |
| 10 | Does the script contain camera directions? | `CLOSE UP on the key. PAN to the door.` | `The key. Behind it, the door.` | [proposal] |
| 11 | Do two characters speak in the same voice, so their lines could be swapped? | Both speak in full, formal sentences | One speaks in fragments; the other never answers directly | [proposal] |
| 12 | Does the ending resolve every conflict with no cost? | Everyone forgives everyone; the storm clears | She is right, and it costs her the job | [F4] |
| 13 | Does an event happen by coincidence to solve the protagonist's problem? | A stranger happens to have the missing map | She trades her boat for the map in S02 | [proposal] |
| 14 | In a fact-based story, is a dramatized element unmarked, or a fact different from the concept? | Invented quote presented as real | `(dramatized)` tag; or quote sourced in concept | [proposal] |
| 15 | Does the estimated runtime miss the target by more than 10 percent? | Target 8 min, estimate 11 min | Target 8 min, estimate 8.5 min | [proposal] |

Verdict format:

```
REJECT #1 — S04, MARA: "I'm scared of being alone." — states the feeling.
```
