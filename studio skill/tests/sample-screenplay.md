# The Fortieth Night

**Logline:** A lighthouse keeper's daughter must keep the lamp burning alone through a week of storms after her father is injured, or the relief boat carrying the doctor will be lost on the rocks.

**Synopsis:** On a remote rock, twelve-year-old Mara tends her injured father, the keeper. The oil is nearly gone and the relief boat is overdue. When the storm returns she climbs the tower, finds the oil spent, and burns what the family owns to keep the light alive. At dawn the boat lands. The light has cost her the one thing she had of her mother.

**Structure note:** A single escalating situation in four scenes. Target runtime 60 seconds.

## Character and world facts

- C01 MARA, 12. Small for her age, cropped dark hair, her father's oilskin coat with the sleeves rolled. Wants to prove she can keep the light. (dramatized)
- C02 TOMAS, 50s, the keeper. Broad, grey beard, left leg splinted. (dramatized)
- L01 LAMP ROOM. Round, glass on all sides, brass lamp at the centre.
- L02 KEEPER'S ROOM. One bed, one stove, one shelf.
- P01 MUSIC BOX. Walnut, a ship carved on the lid. It was Mara's mother's.
- P02 OIL CAN. Dented tin, a painted red band.

## Scenes

S01. INT. KEEPER'S ROOM - NIGHT

Wind against the shutters. Tomas lies on the bed, leg splinted, watching the ceiling. Above them the light sweeps past the window, then dims, then returns.

Mara winds the music box on the shelf. It plays four notes and sticks.

                    TOMAS
          You counted the cans.

                    MARA
          Twice.

She does not say the number. She takes the oil can and goes.

Turn: Tomas knows the oil is nearly gone, and that she will not let him climb.

S02. INT. LAMP ROOM - NIGHT

Rain sheets down the glass. Mara tips the oil can over the lamp. A thin thread, then drops, then nothing. The flame shrinks to a blue bead.

Out in the dark, far off, a small yellow light rises and falls. A boat.

Turn: the oil is gone and the boat is now in sight of the rocks.

S03. INT. KEEPER'S ROOM - NIGHT

Mara pulls the shelf from the wall and breaks it over her knee. She takes the chair. She takes the bed slats from under her father's mattress; he lifts himself to let her.

Her hand stops on the music box. Tomas looks at it, then at her, and says nothing.

Turn: she has decided what the light is worth.

S04. EXT. LANDING - DAWN

Grey calm water. A boat tied at the steps. A doctor climbs with his bag.

At the top of the tower the glass is black with smoke. Mara sits against the rail, asleep, the oilskin over her. In her open hand, the brass comb of the music box, four teeth bent.

Turn: the boat is safe, and the box is gone.
