Version 0.1.0 — 2026-11-13

## Limits of this research (read first)

No source page could be opened in the build session (network restriction). Every finding is a search-engine summary of a page, typed `search summary, page not opened`. None counts as verified. The search itself judged several of these pages to be low-authority.

## Questions

1. How are historical claims verified for narrative work?
2. How do professionals separate fact from dramatization?
3. How often do AI tools fabricate sources, and how is that caught?
4. How is originality checked against existing coverage?
5. What makes a true event a story rather than a topic?
